Logistics & Shipping Overview

Efficient supply chain coordination is vital for delivering agricultural produce safely to global destinations. IndoVel Ventures oversees export logistics, freight planning, and container shipping for international buyer orders.

We coordinate Full Container Load (FCL) and Less than Container Load (LCL) ocean freight, port handling, and inland transportation, providing transparent shipment tracking and continuous communication throughout the delivery cycle.

Working Process

Our logistics and shipping operations coordinate efficient temperature-controlled cold chain transit from major Indian ports to international destinations. We manage FCL and LCL container bookings, port handling, container stuffing, dunnage lining, and real-time shipment tracking.

Everything You Need to Know

  • What ocean freight modes do you support for export shipments?

    We manage Full Container Loads (FCL 20ft/40ft dry & reefer containers) and Less than Container Loads (LCL) based on cargo volume.

  • How is temperature maintained for perishable fruits and vegetables?

    Perishables are transported in climate-controlled refrigerated (reefer) containers with active temperature and humidity monitoring from port to port.

  • Which ports in India do you ship from?

    We ship primarily through major Indian ports including Chennai, JNPT Mumbai, Cochin, Tuticorin, and Visakhapatnam depending on cargo origin and destination.

  • How can buyers track their shipment status during transit?

    We provide real-time container tracking numbers, vessel schedule updates, and estimated arrival notifications throughout voyage transit.

  • What shipping Incoterms do you offer to global buyers?

    We offer standard international shipping terms including FOB (Free on Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), and CFR (Cost and Freight).

  • How do you prevent container cargo damage during sea voyages?

    Containers are professionally stuffed, palletized, dunnage-strapped, and lined with moisture absorbers to prevent shifting and condensation damage.

Faq's

Everything You Need
to Know

  • What core export services does IndoVel Ventures offer?

    We provide end-to-end export solutions comprising Product Sourcing, Quality & Inspection, Custom Packaging, Export Documentation, Logistics & Shipping, Global Market Support, and Buyer-Specific Solutions.

  • Can IndoVel Ventures handle custom product specifications and grading?

    Yes, we work directly with agricultural producers and processing units to source, grade, and package products according to destination-market regulations and buyer requirements.

  • How do you manage phytosanitary and customs clearance?

    Our documentation team manages all mandatory export paperwork, including Phytosanitary Certificates, Certificates of Origin, FSSAI clearances, and commercial invoicing for smooth customs passage.

  • What shipping terms and freight modes do you support?

    We support flexible shipping terms (FOB, CIF, CFR) and coordinate ocean FCL/LCL freight as well as temperature-controlled reefer shipping based on cargo perishability.
